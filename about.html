About Footpad

A whole site about socks. Really.

Because the thing between your foot and your shoe decides how the day feels.

What we do

Footpad explains sock comfort in plain language: how cushioning is made, why some socks stay up and others do not, and how to wash each fibre so it lasts. We publish two guides and three small tools, and keep them accurate.

How we do it

Guides are written from wearing and washing a great many socks, and checked against what knitters and textile care organisations publish. A second reader reviews each one before it goes live.

What we do not do

We do not sell socks, name or rank brands, or accept payment for coverage. We do not give medical advice; if you have a foot condition, please speak to a qualified professional.
